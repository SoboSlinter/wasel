Terms of Service

Version 2026-10 · Last updated October 2026

1. What Wasel is

Wasel is a marketplace that connects customers with independent local service providers. Providers are not employees or agents of Wasel. The service contract is between the customer and the provider.

2. Accounts

You must be at least 18 and give accurate information. Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.

3. Providers

  • New providers are reviewed by Wasel before they appear to customers. To apply you upload an ID and a business license or proof of insurance. Wasel may approve your application, reject it, or ask you for more information or documents, and may suspend a provider at its discretion. Wasel keeps the list of services that can be offered on the platform; you choose from that list and set your own prices, and Wasel may add, change, hide or remove a service on the list at any time.
  • You confirm that you are allowed to do the work you offer, including any licenses, permits or insurance the law requires in the areas you serve.
  • You set your own prices and areas, and you can change a price at any time. A new price applies to bookings made after the change; a booking keeps the price it was made at. You agree to show up, do the work with reasonable care and communicate honestly.
  • Wasel charges a platform fee on each booking (10% on the Free plan, 5% on Wasel Pro). The fee is fixed when a booking is created. Wasel may change these fees or the Pro price; a change applies only to bookings made, and Pro subscriptions started, after it.
  • Wasel Pro is an optional paid plan ($19 per month, billed by Stripe) with a lower platform fee and priority in search. You can manage or cancel it in Stripe's billing portal.
  • Payouts are sent through Stripe after the job is marked completed, the customer has paid and the customer has confirmed the job (see section 5). Payouts can be held or withheld while a payment is disputed or a problem is open.

4. Customers

  • Prices are shown before you book. You pay online by card after the provider confirms.
  • You can cancel a booking until 1 hour before it starts. In the last hour only Wasel can cancel, so contact us if you really need to. If you already paid, the full amount is refunded to your card. Banks can take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • If you ask your bank to reverse a payment (a dispute), Wasel may share information about the booking with Stripe and your bank.

5. Finishing a job

  • When a provider marks a job completed, the customer is asked to confirm that it was done, or to report a problem.
  • If the customer confirms, the provider's payout is released. If the customer does nothing, the job is confirmed automatically 72 hours after it was marked completed, and the payout is released. We send a reminder email about 24 hours before that.
  • If the customer reports a problem, the payout is held until Wasel reviews it. Wasel may release the payout to the provider, or the customer may be refunded, based on what both sides tell us. Wasel's decision on a reported problem is final for the purposes of the platform, and does not limit rights either side has under the law.

6. Payments

Card payments and provider payouts are processed by Stripe. Wasel does not store card numbers. Stripe's terms apply to payments and to provider payout accounts.

7. Acceptable use

No fraud, harassment, illegal services, fake reviews or attempts to bypass Wasel fees on bookings that started on Wasel. We can remove content and accounts that break these rules.

8. Reviews

Customers who completed a booking can review it, with a star rating and an optional comment. After a job is completed and the customer has confirmed it, the provider can also rate the customer. A customer sees only their own average rating; providers see a customer's average rating (not the comments) on their bookings. Ratings must be honest and about the job. A review is shown with the customer's first name and last initial. We may remove reviews that break these rules. If a provider's average rating stays low across many reviews, Wasel will look at the account and, following this policy, may suspend or close it. Wasel decides each case individually; nothing is suspended automatically.

10. Emails, changes and contact

We send emails about your bookings and payments (for example a new request, a confirmation, a refund or a reminder). These are part of the service. We may update these terms and will update the version date above. Questions: contact us.

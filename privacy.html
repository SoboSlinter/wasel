Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10 · Last updated October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored hashed by our login provider, never readable by us).
  • Bookings: the service, date, address and notes you enter, and the booking status.
  • Providers: business name, description, phone, business email, address, service areas, business type, categories, years of experience, languages, website, and photos. License or insurance details, the documents you upload (your ID, license, proof of insurance and other papers you choose to add), how you heard about us and the date you accepted the terms are private: documents are stored in a private folder and only you and Wasel administrators can open them.
  • Reviews and problem reports: the star rating and comment you write, and the note you write if you report a problem with a job.
  • Messages to us: the name, email, topic and message you send through the Contact page, and your account if you were signed in.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We store the amount, status and Stripe reference numbers, not your card number. Providers give identity and bank details directly to Stripe to receive payouts.

What other people can see

  • Provider pages are public: business name, description, city, rating, service areas, categories, languages, website and photos.
  • A provider's phone, email and address are shown on their public page. Customers see a provider's phone only after a booking is confirmed.
  • A review shows your star rating, comment, date and your first name with last initial. Problem reports are seen only by the provider involved and Wasel administrators.
  • Providers see the customer's name, phone, address and notes for their own bookings only.

Emails

We email you about your bookings and payments (new request, confirmed, declined, cancelled, refund, job done, a reminder before automatic confirmation, a reported problem). We use an email delivery provider for this. We do not send marketing email.

Cookies and storage

Wasel keeps you signed in and remembers your light or dark choice using your browser's own storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are served from Wasel itself, not from a third party.

How we use it

To run bookings and payments, review providers, prevent fraud, handle disputes and refunds, and improve Wasel. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run Wasel: Supabase (hosting, database, login), Stripe (payments) and our email delivery provider (booking emails). We may share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app. To delete your account or ask for a copy of your data, use the Contact page and choose "Privacy or deleting my data". We keep payment records as long as the law requires.

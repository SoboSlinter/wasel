About Wasel

Wasel connects people with trusted local service providers: car detailing, home cleaning, handyman work and computer repair.

Why we exist

Finding someone reliable for a small job should not take a week of calls and guesswork. Wasel puts local providers, clear prices and honest reviews in one place, so you know who is coming, what it costs and what happens if plans change.

How we keep it fair

Providers are reviewed

Every provider is checked by our team before they appear in search.

You pay after they confirm

Nothing is charged until the provider accepts your booking. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your card number.

Full refund if cancelled

Cancel before the job starts, or if the provider declines, and the whole amount goes back to your card.

You confirm the job

After the work is done you confirm it, or report a problem. The provider is paid once you confirm, or automatically after 72 hours if you do not reply. If you report a problem, the payout waits until our team has looked at it.

Reviews from real jobs

Only customers who completed a booking can leave a star rating and a comment.

Fair fees for providers

Providers keep 90% of each booking on the Free plan, or 95% with Wasel Pro. They set their own prices and the areas they serve.

Where we work

Wasel is built for Texas, starting in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. Providers choose the cities and ZIP codes they serve, and search shows only the ones that work near you.
